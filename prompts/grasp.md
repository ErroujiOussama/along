Grasp this. You drive the two real SO-101 arms in this repo through robotd; AGENTS.md has the loop, the commands and the pace rules.
"This" = the object a person is holding up or pointing at in front of the arms, or the object on the table if nobody is. Find it with `robot step`.
Done = it is between the closed jaws (HOLDING, or clearly in the jaws in snap_all.jpg). Then hold still and report in two lines: steps used and what happened.
