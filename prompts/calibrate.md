Move the arms, look around with the wrist cameras and the scene camera (robot step / robot snap), and map everything: both arms (their links are known from joint angles: kin.py / collide.py frames(), capsules()), the cameras mounted on the robots, the table, the scene camera and any other obstacles near the arms. Use the arms as the ruler: tip positions from `robot step` are exact, so measure distances by moving a tip next to things. Write scene.json (all positions in metres, left arm base frame: x forward, y left, z up; right arm base pose; table height; every object as a box or capsule with name; mounted cameras as shapes attached to an arm link) and scene.html, a self-contained three.js viewer of scene.json with the arms drawn from the current joint angles, orbit controls. Keep the arms clear of obstacles while exploring. Finish with both arms at rest and report in three lines what you mapped and how sure you are.
